## PARTIAL or MISSING

1. **3.2: the calibration split's purpose is not logged.** The log says validation is for model selection only; nothing records that calibration is reserved for fitting calibration in Step 7.
2. **3.3: two same-day baseline counts disagree.** Log row 89 gives 35,888 scored sessions with same-day baseline sessions; the leakage report gives 31,660. Both give 0.7%. The rule itself matches the features.yaml comment.
3. **3.4: not every stage calls `verify_test_checksum()` first.** The build stages upstream of the freeze (data, sessions, features, features_text, labels) do not, nor do explore_positives and prep_report. splits verifies only after building. Every stage after the freeze (xgb, rules, eval, eval-baselines) calls it first.
4. **3.6: `rules_methods.md` lacks the prior-knowledge sentence.** It is only in docs/methods_notes.md and the log.

Nothing is MISSING.
