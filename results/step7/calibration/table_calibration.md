# Calibration (Step 7.1): before and after, calib and test

Fitted on calib only. Mean ± std over seeds 1-3 and 95% user-level bootstrap CI (1,000 resamples, stratified, seed 0), src/eval.py --calibration. ECE with 15 bins. Session = the primary unit (C1: max over scoring points; C2: the logoff row); row = C1's secondary view, with its own row-level fit. 'Mean predicted' vs 'observed' is the prior-shift diagnostic. Brier skill score BSS = 1 − Brier / (p(1 − p)): primary p = the calib positive rate at the same unit (known at deployment), secondary p = the evaluated split's own rate. Rules are not calibrated (Brier and ECE n/a).

Platt Brier ≈ climatology: calibrated probabilities carry little skill beyond the base rate; ranking metrics (Tables 1–2) carry the signal.
